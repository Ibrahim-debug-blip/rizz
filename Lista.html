List A Career of Muhammad Rizwan

listAMohammad Rizwan boasts an outstanding List A (one-day domestic and international) career, where he has firmly established himself as a highly dependable anchor and a prolific run-scorer. Averaging a stellar 47.04 across more than 200 matches, his white-ball prowess consistently pushed his case for national selection. A defining early-career milestone came during the Pentangular One Day Cup final, where he smashed a brilliant 103 off just 87 balls to steer the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Fighters to a 6-wicket victory over the Baluchistan Warriors. Known for his relentless strike-rotation and incredible fitness, he has accumulated over 6,800 List A runs, combining his extensive domestic campaigns for teams like Sui Northern Gas Pipelines Limited (SNGPL) with his international appearances for the Pakistan national team.

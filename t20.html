T20 Career of Muhammad Rizwan

Mohammad Rizwan has built a monumental profile in overall Twenty20 cricket, establishing himself as one of the most consistent anchors in the format's history. Across 312 T20 matches on the global circuit, he has accumulated over 9,000 career runs at an exceptional average of 41.08.His career completely transformed when he was promoted to open the batting, leading to a historic 2021 campaign where he became the only player in cricket history to score over 2,000 T20 runs in a single calendar year. Beyond international duties, Rizwan is a highly sought-after franchise asset, prominently leading the Multan Sultans as captain to their maiden Pakistan Super League (PSL) title in 2021. He has also displayed his clinical stroke-play and agile wicketkeeping globally while representing various domestic franchises like Comilla Victorians, Sussex, and Lahore Qalandars.

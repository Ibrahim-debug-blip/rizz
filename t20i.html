T20I Career of Muhammad Rizwan

Mohammad Rizwan has built an iconic legacy in Twenty20 Internationals (T20Is), widely celebrated for his incredible consistency and historic partnerships. After making his debut in 2015 against Bangladesh, his career reached legendary heights when he was promoted to open the batting alongside Babar Azam.In a historic 2021 calendar year, Rizwan put together the most prolific run in short-format history, shattering the record for the most T20I runs in a single calendar year with 1,326 runs at a staggering average of 73.66, which earned him the prestigious ICC Men’s T20I Cricketer of the Year award. He is also one of the rare elite batsmen globally to record a century in all three international formats, highlighted by his unbeaten 104* against South Africa. Across 106 T20I matches, he has accumulated 3,414 runs at an elite average of 47.42, solidifying his role as a pillar of Pakistan’s white-ball cricket.
